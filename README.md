# Beyond an MBA: Building IIT Gandhinagar's Management Ecosystem

**Case Study | Sthapana Divas | Consulting Club, IIT Gandhinagar**

**Presented by:** Ashwini Mane & Sunkara Anuradha

---

## 📌 Overview

This case study explores how IIT Gandhinagar can enter the management education space while leveraging its strengths in technology, interdisciplinary learning, innovation, research, and entrepreneurship.

The objective was to develop a strategic pathway and implementation blueprint that enables IITGN to build a differentiated management ecosystem while managing financial, operational, and reputational risks.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendation

We propose a **phased approach** rather than an immediate full-fledged management degree.

### The 3C Model: Culture, Credentials & Career

Our strategic roadmap consists of four stages:

**1. Culture First**  
Build a strong management culture and ecosystem within the institute.

**2. Course & Certification Expansion**  
Introduce specialized courses and certifications to validate demand and generate early revenue.

**3. Industry Partnerships**  
Develop strong industry collaborations to improve curriculum relevance, credibility, and career opportunities.

**4. Full-Fledged Degree Program**  
Launch an innovative management degree program after establishing a validated foundation.

---

## 🎯 Why This Strategy?

The phased approach aims to:

- Build student demand before large-scale investment
- Minimize implementation and reputational risk
- Develop industry relevance through continuous collaboration
- Generate early revenue through executive education and certifications
- Establish a differentiated management identity for IITGN

---

## 🔍 Key Strategic Areas

### Competitive Benchmarking

The analysis evaluates IITGN's potential positioning against established management institutions and technology-focused B-schools, including IIMs and leading IIT management schools.

### Program Positioning

The proposed positioning combines:

**Technology + Engineering + Management + Industry**

The goal is to create a management education model that integrates technical expertise with business and managerial decision-making.

### Business Model

Key components include:

- Technology-driven management education
- Industry-specific interdisciplinary programs
- AI-enabled and future-ready curriculum
- Executive education and certifications
- Corporate training and consulting
- Degree programs in the later stage

### Industry-Aligned Curriculum

Potential specialization areas include:

- AI & Technology
- Healthcare
- Sustainability
- Manufacturing

Example modules include AI for Business, Product Management, Digital Transformation, ESG Strategy, Smart Manufacturing, and Supply Chain Analytics.

### Implementation Strategy

The proposed ecosystem includes:

- Annual Management Festival
- Consulting Clubs
- Case Study Competitions
- CXO Leadership Series
- Executive Bootcamps
- Innovation Hub
- Global Management Week

---

## 🗺️ Academic Roadmap

The roadmap progressively moves from ecosystem building and short-format learning to industry integration and, eventually, a full-fledged management degree.

This sequencing allows IITGN to validate demand, strengthen capabilities, and build institutional credibility before making a larger commitment.
